🏢 HR Data Analysis Dashboard 📊
Project Overview:
This Power BI HR Dashboard provides comprehensive insights into key HR metrics, empowering HR managers and teams to monitor employee attrition, satisfaction, and demographics. The dashboard helps organizations make data-driven decisions to reduce attrition, improve employee satisfaction, and optimize workforce management.

🔑 Key Metrics & Visualizations:
👥 Total Employees: Displays the total number of employees in the organization.
📉 Attrition & Attrition Rate: Tracks the number of employees leaving and the overall attrition rate over time.
💼 Active Employees: Monitors the current number of active employees.
🎂 Average Age of Employees: Provides insights into the workforce's average age for demographic analysis.
🏢 Department-wise Attrition: Visualizes attrition trends across departments to spot areas needing attention.
📊 Employees by Age Group: Breaks down employee count by age group for workforce structure analysis.
😊 Job Satisfaction Rating: Analyzes employee satisfaction based on survey ratings, offering insights into employee morale.
📈 Attrition Rate by Gender & Age Group: Offers a detailed breakdown of attrition rates based on gender and age group to identify retention challenges.

🛠️ Skills Used:
Power BI: Built dynamic, interactive dashboards for HR insights.
DAX Functions: Leveraged advanced DAX calculations for key HR metrics.
Data Modeling & Cleaning: Prepared and structured HR data for accurate reporting and analysis.
HR KPI Dashboards: Tracked and visualized critical HR metrics for effective decision-making.
This dashboard is an essential tool for HR professionals, providing a clear view of workforce trends and enabling better employee retention strategies and workforce planning. By leveraging interactive data visualizations, HR teams can take proactive steps to enhance employee satisfaction and reduce attrition.
